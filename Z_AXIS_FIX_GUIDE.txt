================================================================================
Z-AXIS FIX - SIMPLE STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Z-axis (pen up/down) is slow and not coming down properly.

SOLUTION: We made the Z-axis faster and stronger.

================================================================================
STEP 1: UPLOAD THE FIXED FIRMWARE
================================================================================

1. Open Arduino IDE
2. Open file: diy cnc/arduino_plotter_smooth.ino
3. Click "Upload" button
4. Wait for "Done uploading" message

This firmware now has:
- Z moves 2x faster (was 1500 µs/step, now 800 µs/step)
- Z travels further (was 250 steps, now 400 steps)
- Z stays powered when pen is down (holds pressure on paper)

================================================================================
STEP 2: TEST Z-AXIS IN THE APP
================================================================================

1. Open the Neuro Plot app (main.py)
2. Click "Connect" button
3. Go to Settings tab
4. Scroll down to "Calibration Tools" section
5. Look for "Z Jog (Fine Tune)" buttons

Test 1: Press "Z Down (-10)" button
- The pen should move DOWN quickly
- If it moves UP instead, press "Flip Direction" button once
- Then press "Z Down (-10)" again - it should now move DOWN

Test 2: Press "Z Up (+10)" button
- The pen should move UP quickly
- It should be noticeably faster than before

Test 3: Press "Z Down (-10)" multiple times
- The pen should move down smoothly
- If it shakes or vibrates, press "Fix Shaking (Swap Phases)" button
- Then test again

================================================================================
STEP 3: CALIBRATE PEN LIFT DISTANCE
================================================================================

1. In Settings tab, find "Pen Lift Distance (steps)" slider
2. Current value is 400 (we increased it from 250)
3. Test with current value:
   - Press "Z Down (-10)" to lower pen to paper
   - Press "Z Up (+10)" to lift pen off paper
   - Pen should lift completely off the paper

4. If pen doesn't reach paper:
   - Increase slider to 500, 600, 700, etc.
   - Click "Apply All Calibration"
   - Test again with Z Jog buttons

5. If pen lifts too high (wastes time):
   - Decrease slider to 350, 300, etc.
   - Click "Apply All Calibration"
   - Test again

Find the sweet spot where:
- Pen DOWN touches paper with good pressure
- Pen UP clears paper completely

================================================================================
STEP 4: TEST DRAWING
================================================================================

1. Select an image or enter text
2. Click "Start Drawing"
3. Watch the pen:
   - Should move DOWN quickly before drawing
   - Should move UP quickly after drawing
   - Should NOT be slow or stuck

If pen still doesn't come down:
- Go back to Step 2 and press "Flip Direction"
- Test again

If pen shakes:
- Go back to Step 2 and press "Fix Shaking (Swap Phases)"
- Test again

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Problem: Pen still moves slowly
Solution: 
  1. Go to Settings
  2. Find "Z Jog" buttons
  3. Press "Extra Torque (Mode)" button
  4. Test with Z Jog buttons again

Problem: Pen moves wrong direction (up instead of down)
Solution:
  1. Go to Settings
  2. Find "Z Jog" buttons
  3. Press "Flip Direction" button
  4. Test with Z Jog buttons again

Problem: Pen shakes or vibrates
Solution:
  1. Go to Settings
  2. Find "Z Jog" buttons
  3. Press "Fix Shaking (Swap Phases)" button
  4. Test with Z Jog buttons again

Problem: Pen doesn't reach paper
Solution:
  1. Go to Settings
  2. Find "Pen Lift Distance (steps)" slider
  3. Increase value (try 500, 600, 700)
  4. Click "Apply All Calibration"
  5. Test with Z Jog buttons
